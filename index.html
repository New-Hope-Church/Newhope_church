"If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation."

2 Corinthians 5:17

At New Hope Church, we are a community of people learning to follow Jesus, grow together, and live out His love in everyday life. We gather to worship, learn from God’s Word, pray, care for one another, and serve our communities with compassion.

We believe the hope we have in Christ is not just something we hold onto, but something we are called to share. Through the gospel, genuine relationships, practical care, and a life of faith, we desire to see people know Jesus, grow in Him, and become a blessing to others and to Nepal.
