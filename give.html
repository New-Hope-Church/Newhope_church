Giving is part of our life together.

God has given to us generously, and we want our lives to reflect that generosity. We give freely and joyfully not because we are pressured to give, but because we are grateful for what we have received. Whether through our time, gifts, skills, resources, or care for others, every act of generosity can become a way to love our neighbours, strengthen our community, and take part in what God is doing through New Hope.
