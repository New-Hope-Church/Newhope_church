Youth Camp Registration

Worship, Bible study, and teaching for teens and young adults — with meals and accommodation included. Reserve a spot below.

When

9–11 Nov 2026

Three days: Monday 9 to Wednesday 11 November.

Where

Han Kook Inn

Balkumari, Lalitpur

Who

Teens & young adults

Open to teens and young adults.

Before you register

A few things to know before you send your registration in.

What to bring

  • A Bible and notebook
  • Comfortable clothes you can move in
  • Any personal medication you need

What's included

  • Meals throughout the camp
  • Accommodation with bedding provided
  • Worship, Bible studies, and teaching sessions

Good to know

  • Registration fee: Rs. 1,500 per person.
  • Your place is confirmed once the fee is received.
  • How to pay: fill in the form and we will email you the payment QR code from the church's official email. After paying, come back here, upload a screenshot of the payment confirmation, and submit the form.
  • Questions? Email newhopekavre@gmail.com or visit our Connect page.
