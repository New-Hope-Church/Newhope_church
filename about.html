Hope isn't a feeling we chase. It's a person we follow.

A community of God-loving people, growing in Christ and living in one unshakeable hope that in Christ, every broken thing can be made new. Come as you are.

Who we are

A community of God-loving people, anchored in one unshakeable hope and gathered as one body in Christ. We’re a diverse congregation of families from across Nepal, coming together to worship, learn, and care for one another. We seek to live out God’s Great Commission, discover His calling for our lives, and serve others with radical servanthood.

Whether you grew up in church or you're walking through the door for the first time, you'll find people glad you came.

Our beliefs

Faith shapes how we live. At New Hope, we are centered on Jesus, grounded in Scripture, and called to live with hope, purpose, and love.

The good news

Jesus makes all things new. Jesus lived, died, and rose again so that we can know God, receive forgiveness, and find new life in Him. His grace is the hope we carry into every part of life.

Scripture

God speaks, and we listen. The Bible is God’s Word and the foundation of our faith. It shapes how we know God, understand His story, make decisions, and live faithfully in the world.

Life Together

Faith was never meant to be lived alone. We grow through genuine relationships, shared worship, honest conversations, and serving one another. At New Hope, we make room for people to belong, grow, and walk with Jesus together.

Our Mission

Love God. Love people. Bring hope. We believe the church is sent beyond its walls—to serve our neighbours, care for our community, share the good news of Jesus, and live out His love in everyday life.

Our values

Three commitments shape how we worship, belong, and live as the people of New Hope.

Worship

Authentic worship

We make space to genuinely meet with God not to perform for one another, but to respond to Him with honest hearts.

Community

Real community

We follow Jesus together, encouraging one another, carrying each others burdens, and growing in faith and love.

Service

Faith in action

We put our faith into practice by serving others, caring for our community, and using what we've been given to make a difference.

Leadership

Rev. Ruben Rana Magar

Ruben has led New Hope Church for over a decade, alongside his wife and a team of local elders. His preaching centres on the hope found in Christ, and his door is open to anyone in the congregation who wants to talk, pray, or simply be heard.
